Signal quality

  1. Sit as you would to play, about an arm's length from the screen.
  2. Jitter: keep your head still and stare at one spot for 3 seconds.
  3. Range: keep your head still and look from the far left edge of the screen to the far right, then top to bottom, for 6 seconds.

Guide: range ÷ jitter of at least 10 horizontally and 6 vertically suggests the 150 px accuracy target is reachable on a typical laptop screen.
